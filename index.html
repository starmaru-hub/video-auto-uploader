Video Auto Uploader is a video publishing tool for creators who post the same content across multiple platforms. Instead of uploading a video one platform at a time, creators upload once and Video Auto Uploader publishes it to every platform they've connected — under each creator's own account, using each platform's official login.

Features

  • Multi-platform publishing — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Facebook, X (Twitter), and Naver Clip from a single upload.
  • Per-user account connections — every user signs in to their own TikTok (and other platform) accounts via the platform's standard OAuth login. We never use a shared or developer-owned account to publish on a user's behalf.
  • Per-platform captions — set a shared title/description/hashtags, or customize wording for each platform individually.
  • Auto first-comment — optionally post a first comment automatically after the video goes live.
  • Upload status tracking — see the live status of each platform's upload (queued, uploading, done, or error) as it happens.

How It Works

1.Create an account and connect the platforms you post to. For TikTok, this uses TikTok Login Kit — you're redirected to TikTok's own login page and asked to authorize Video Auto Uploader for your account.
2.Upload a video once, and write a title, description, and hashtags (shared across platforms, or customized per platform).
3.Video Auto Uploader publishes the video to each connected platform under your own account, and shows you the result and links to each published post.

Who It's For

Video Auto Uploader is built for individual creators, small teams, and agencies who manage several social accounts and want to save time by publishing once instead of repeating the same upload on every platform.
